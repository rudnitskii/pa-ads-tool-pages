Data Deletion Instructions

The application "DevTools" is an internal agency tool and does not store personal data of Facebook or Instagram users. It only processes advertising data of accounts it has been granted access to.

How to request deletion

To remove the application's access to your data: in Facebook go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find "DevTools" and click Remove.

To request deletion of any data we hold about you or your ad account, email rudnitski.ruslan@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We will delete the data and confirm within 30 days.
