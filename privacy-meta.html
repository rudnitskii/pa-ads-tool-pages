Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

This Privacy Policy describes how the application "DevTools" ("the application") accesses and handles data. The application is an internal tool used exclusively by the performance marketing agency PA to manage and report on advertising accounts of its own clients.

Data we access

Through the Meta Marketing API the application reads and manages advertising data of ad accounts, Pages and product catalogs that the authorized user manages or has been granted access to: campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, budgets, performance metrics (impressions, clicks, spend, conversions) and catalog items. The application does not collect personal data of Facebook or Instagram end users and does not use Facebook Login for the public.

How we use the data

Data is used solely for reporting, auditing and optimizing advertising campaigns on behalf of the owners of the accounts. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising profiling or any other purpose.

Data storage and retention

Data is processed on the agency's own devices and is not published. Reports are kept only as long as needed to service the client and are deleted on request.
